# AI/ML Internship — Day 14

## Team Task: Cloth Simulation Research

### Objective

The objective of this task was to research modern cloth simulation technologies used in Virtual Try-On Systems, AR/VR applications, gaming environments, digital fashion platforms, and character animation systems. The study focused on understanding how virtual garments interact with human body models, respond to movement, handle collisions, and maintain realistic fabric behavior.

# Conclusion

Day 14 focused on researching modern cloth simulation technologies used in Virtual Try-On systems, digital fashion platforms, and AR/VR environments. The study evaluated Unity Cloth System, NVIDIA PhysX, Marvelous Designer, and Blender Cloth Physics based on realism, performance, collision handling, and implementation feasibility.

The research concluded that a combination of Blender, Unity, and NVIDIA PhysX provides the most balanced solution for developing scalable and realistic Virtual Try-On systems while maintaining high performance and cost efficiency.

This task strengthened understanding of cloth simulation workflows, physics-based garment behavior, digital fashion technologies, and real-time Virtual Try-On architectures.
