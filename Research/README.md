# Research

## Overview

This folder contains all research activities, reports, comparisons, findings, and documentation completed during the Raritone Summer Internship 2026.

The research focused on Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Virtual Try-On Systems, Human Body Reconstruction, 3D Avatar Generation, and emerging technologies used in digital fashion applications.
