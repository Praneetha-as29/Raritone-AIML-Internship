# AI/ML Internship — Team Collaboration and Business Workflow Study

## AI-Powered Fashion Commerce Platform

### Final Internship Research

---

## Project Description

This repository presents a study of the collaborative workflow followed by different technical departments involved in the development of an AI-based fashion commerce platform.

The research focuses on understanding how individual teams contribute to the complete product lifecycle and how information flows between various modules of the system.

---

## Purpose of the Study

- Understand the complete project ecosystem.
- Study inter-team communication.
- Analyze business workflow integration.
- Explore the relationship between AI services and platform operations.
- Learn how data supports intelligent decision making.

---

### Artificial Intelligence Team

## Major Observations

- AI services depend heavily on backend infrastructure.
- Business analytics improve platform performance.
- Customer data supports AI model optimization.
- Collaboration between teams increases system efficiency.
- Shared technologies simplify development and maintenance.

---

## Research Summary

The study demonstrated that successful AI applications require contributions from multiple engineering disciplines rather than isolated development efforts. Backend services, Artificial Intelligence, and Data Analytics collectively create a unified platform capable of delivering intelligent and personalized user experiences.
