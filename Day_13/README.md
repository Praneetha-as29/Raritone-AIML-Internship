# Day 13 – 3D Modeling & Assets Team

## Overview

The focus of Day 13 was to build a structured 3D asset repository for future Virtual Try-On development. The work involved collecting fashion-related assets, organizing datasets into meaningful categories, exploring human avatar resources, and gaining hands-on experience with Blender for 3D asset visualization.

---

## Objectives

- Collect 3D clothing assets
- Collect human avatar resources
- Organize datasets into structured folders
- Explore Blender for 3D asset visualization
- Prepare a reusable asset repository for future Virtual Try-On development
