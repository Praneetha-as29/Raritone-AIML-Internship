# AI/ML Internship — Day 16

## Pose Detection Accuracy Analysis

### Objective

Research and analyze pose detection models used for human body landmark identification. The study focused on evaluating landmark detection accuracy under different body poses, lighting conditions, and viewing angles.

---

## Models Studied

### MediaPipe Pose
- Real-time pose estimation framework
- Detects 33 body landmarks
- Lightweight and fast
- Suitable for mobile and web applications

### OpenPose
- Deep learning-based pose estimation model
- Supports body, hand, and facial landmark detection
- High landmark accuracy
- Suitable for research and advanced applications

---

## Experimental Conditions

The models were analyzed under:

- Front View
- Side View
- Arms Raised
- Different Body Poses
- Low Lighting Conditions
- Complex Backgrounds

---

## Landmarks Evaluated

- Shoulders
- Elbows
- Wrists
- Hips
- Knees
- Ankles

---

## Comparison Results

| Criteria | MediaPipe Pose | OpenPose |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| Speed | Excellent | Good |
| Accuracy | Good | Excellent |
| Side View Detection | Good | Very Good |
| Multi-Person Detection | Limited | Excellent |
| Hardware Requirements | Low | High |
| Real-Time Performance | Excellent | Good |

---

## Key Findings

- MediaPipe Pose provides fast real-time landmark detection.
- OpenPose delivers higher landmark accuracy.
- MediaPipe is better suited for lightweight applications.
- OpenPose performs better in complex pose estimation tasks.
- Both models are suitable for Virtual Try-On systems.

## Conclusion

This study evaluated MediaPipe Pose and OpenPose for human body landmark detection. MediaPipe offers excellent speed and deployment flexibility, while OpenPose provides superior accuracy and robustness. The findings help identify suitable pose estimation solutions for future Virtual Try-On and Computer Vision applications
