# AI/ML Internship — Day 17

## 3D Avatar Generation & Body Reconstruction Research

### Objective
Research modern 3D human body reconstruction and avatar generation techniques used in Virtual Try-On systems, AR/VR applications, digital humans, and fashion technology.

---

## Technologies Studied

### SMPL
- Parametric 3D human body model
- Generates realistic body meshes
- Widely used in Virtual Try-On systems

### PIFuHD
- High-resolution 3D reconstruction from a single image
- Captures body shape and clothing details
- Suitable for digital avatar generation

### ICON
- Clothed human reconstruction framework
- Improves garment and body shape modeling
- Produces realistic avatar outputs

### HumanNeRF
- Neural rendering-based human reconstruction
- Generates dynamic and realistic digital humans
- Supports multi-view synthesis

### 3D Gaussian Splatting
- Real-time 3D scene and avatar rendering
- High visual quality and fast performance
- Emerging technology for interactive applications

## Key Findings

- SMPL provides a strong foundation for body modeling.
- PIFuHD generates detailed avatars from a single image.
- ICON improves clothed human reconstruction quality.
- HumanNeRF offers highly realistic digital human rendering.
- 3D Gaussian Splatting enables real-time photorealistic visualization.
